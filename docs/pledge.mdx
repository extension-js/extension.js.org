Extension.js is an open source framework with a company sponsor. This page says what that means for you, in terms you can hold the project to.

## The pledge

- **MIT licensed, and it stays that way.** You can use, change, and ship Extension.js in any project, commercial or not.
- **It ends at packaging.** A build gives you a folder you can load in a browser, and `build --zip` gives you a zip you can upload to any store yourself.
- **Nothing on your machine needs an account.** `create`, `dev`, `build`, `preview`, and `start` need no sign-in, no key, and no payment.
- **It works without extension.dev.** The platform is optional. Only one command, [`publish`](/docs/commands/publish), talks to it, and only when you run it.
- **extension.dev sponsors the project.** The company behind the platform pays for the framework's maintenance.

## Who is behind it

[extension.dev](https://www.extension.dev?utm_source=extension-js-org&utm_medium=sponsor&utm_campaign=pledge) is a separate product from the same author. It picks up where the framework stops: previews, releases, and store submissions. You never need it to build an extension with Extension.js.

## If the pledge is broken

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/extension-js/extension.js/issues) and quote the line. A broken pledge is a bug.
