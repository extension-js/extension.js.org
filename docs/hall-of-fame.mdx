Contributors across the framework, the docs site, and the surrounding developer experience shape both Extension.js and `extension.js.org` as open-source efforts.

You can contribute in different ways: compiler and runtime work, documentation, examples, design, feedback loops, and funding. This page recognizes contributors to both the framework and the documentation site, and the sponsors and backers who fund the work.

## Become a sponsor

Every tier includes the tiers below it. Recurring sponsorships earn placement, one-time gifts are thanked in BACKERS.md. Sponsorship does not include event promotion or promotional campaigns.

| Tier | Monthly | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backer | $5 | Name in BACKERS.md and on this page |
| Generous Backer | $25 | Name and link, listed above other backers |
| Bronze Sponsor | $50 | Logo and link on this page and in BACKERS.md |
| Silver Sponsor | $200 | Logo and link on the front page of extension.js.org and in the README on GitHub and npm |
| Gold Sponsor | $500 | Larger front page tile, priority issue triage, posts from @extensionjs when you ship something extension developers should know about |
| Platinum Sponsor | $1,000 | Top position and label, a co-built example in extension-js/examples, a quarterly roadmap call, one post per quarter with your copy |
| Partner | by agreement | One exclusive "In partnership with" placement and integration work agreed together |

Sponsor at [github.com/sponsors/cezaraugusto](https://github.com/sponsors/cezaraugusto). After you sponsor, send a light and a dark SVG of your logo and the link you want, the welcome email explains where.
